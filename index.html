SECTOR 01 • WHO WE ARE

About VELOX Racing Team

Born in the mechatronics engineering halls of Zagazig National University, VELOX is a passionate student team engineering Egypt’s next-generation Formula Student single-seater prototype from scratch.

VELOX — from Latin for “swift, agile, rapid” — represents our dedication to building a competitive racing prototype from zero through relentless technical execution and student teamwork.

Formula Student UK is the world’s most prestigious collegiate engineering competition, organized by the Institution of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE) at Silverstone Circuit. We are entering both the Concept Class (Design, Cost, Business) and Dynamics Class.

Our team brings together over 35 ambitious students from Zagazig National University. From laser-notched chromoly tube welding and aerodynamics CFD to ECU telemetry and sponsorship management, every part is engineered by students.
